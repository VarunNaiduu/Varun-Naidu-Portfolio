      Technical SEO explorer

      How I approach three parts of technical SEO. The code snippets are generic demonstrations, not files from a client or employer. The evidence lines describe work reported in the Technical SEO case study.

      Structured data (JSON-LD)

      Objective: describe a page's entities in a form search engines can read.

      Method: pick the schema.org type that matches the visible content, mark up only what the page shows, then check the syntax and test it in Google's Rich Results Test.

      {"@context":"https://schema.org","@type":"Person","name":"Example Name","jobTitle":"Example Title"}

      Demonstration snippet

      Evidence: I implemented JSON-LD on the Recaho POS website. No outcome is attributed to this change alone.

      Crawlability and robots.txt

      Objective: let crawlers reach the pages that matter and skip the ones that don't.

      Method: review the current robots.txt, crawl the site to find blocked or unreachable URLs, then test changes before they go live.

      User-agent: *
Disallow: /admin/
Sitemap: https://www.example.com/sitemap.xml

      Demonstration snippet

      Evidence: I fixed crawlability issues and updated robots.txt on the Recaho POS website. Search performance figures are site-level and are not credited to this change alone.

      Internal linking and Search Console reporting

      Objective: connect related pages and track how they appear in search.

      Method: link related pages with descriptive anchor text, then review queries, pages and impressions in Google Search Console.

      Evidence: I strengthened internal linking on the Recaho POS website. The Selected results figures come from Search Console reporting for that site and period.
